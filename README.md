Undergrad in **Data Science at Waterloo**, currently at **Shopify**. Most of what I
build watches a signal and decides whether it matters — vessel and aircraft traffic
over the Canadian Arctic, acoustic outputs of vocal-fold models, environmental
conditions on a worksite. The rest is full-stack glue to make those answers legible
to someone who isn't me.

```text
now      →  Argus — vessel + air domain awareness for Arctic communities, with NTI
research →  FluidFlow — ML surrogates for vocal-fold acoustics (UW Fluid Flow Physics)
tinker   →  agent tooling, browser automation, anything with a feedback loop
```
